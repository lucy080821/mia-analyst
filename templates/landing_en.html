Frequently Asked Questions

How to connect Telegram for automated reporting? ▼

You simply configure the schedule in the Automation section of the Dashboard and connect the Bot Token. Mia will automatically scan data and send analysis charts directly to your Telegram group on a daily or weekly basis.

Is my data secure and private? ▼

Data safety is our #1 priority. We use AES-256 encryption and enterprise-grade multi-layer security. Mia commits not to share your data with third parties and not to use this data to train public AIs.

Can I use it if I don't know about technical stuff or SQL? ▼

Absolutely! Mia AI Data Analytics is designed to serve non-technical users. You just need to ask in natural language as if talking to an assistant, Mia will automatically handle all complex calculations for you.
