About Mia

Mia SCM - Trợ lý Chuỗi cung ứng is an intelligent virtual assistant designed to help you understand and extract maximum value from your business data without needing to code. We use the latest large language models (LLMs) to "comprehend" tables and answer your questions.

💡

Pro Tip:

You can ask Mia in any language you prefer. Mia supports over 100 languages including Vietnamese, English, Japanese...

Account Setup

It takes less than 30 seconds to get started:

  1. Click the "Get Started" button on the homepage.
  2. Provide your email and set a secure password.
  3. Activate your account via the email sent (if required).
  4. Access the Dashboard to begin your experience.

Upload Your First Data

Mia supports the most common file formats:

Excel
.xlsx, .xls
CSV
Comma/Semicolon delimited
SQL
Direct Connection

In the "Data Analysis" section, simply drag and drop your file into the upload area. Mia will immediately classify data columns (Date, Currency, Category...) to prepare for chatting.

Chat with AI

Instead of writing complex formulas, ask as if you're talking to a colleague:

You ask:

"Tell me the total revenue by month for 2023 and display it as a line chart."

Mia replies:

"Certainly. The total revenue for 2023 is 2.5 billion VND. Here is the detailed monthly chart..."

Data Pipeline

Centrally manage your data sources to provide unified information for AI and Dashboards:

  • Multi-Dataset Support: Connect and consolidate multiple datasets into a single system.
  • Processing & Cleaning: Automatically standardize data formats (dates, metrics, text) before analysis.
  • Automated Sync: Update data in real-time or on a pre-set schedule, ensuring reports always reflect the latest information.

Manage Charts (Dashboard Builder)

Mia provides a professional Visual Dashboard Builder to help you construct custom dashboards:

  • Drag-and-Drop: Intuitively create and arrange chart widgets (Cards, Tables, Scatter, Histogram).
  • Global Time Filters: Apply time filters to synchronously update all widgets on the dashboard.
  • Flexible Customization: Modify data sources, aggregation functions, and visualization types directly via the interface.
⭐

Account Limits:

Free and Plus accounts have hard limits on the number of Dashboards and Widgets. Upgrade for an unlimited experience!
