Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

! Our Commitment

At Mia AI Data Analytics, we value your privacy above all else. Your business data is your most precious asset, and our goal is to protect it using the most advanced security technologies.

1. Information Collection

We only collect necessary information to provide you with the best service:

  • Account information: Name, email, and encrypted password.
  • Uploaded data: Excel, CSV files you actively upload for analysis.
  • Billing information: Processed and secured by partnered payment gateways, we do not store your credit card information.

2. Data Usage

Your data is solely used for the following purposes:

  • Fulfill your analytical reporting requests.
  • Improve the accuracy of AI responses for your account specifically.
  • Send notifications about your account and important system updates.

Save ý quan trọng: Chúng tôi tuyệt đối không sử dụng dữ liệu kinh doanh của khách hàng để huấn luyện các mô hình AI công khai.

3. Security and Storage

We apply enterprise-grade security standards:

  • End-to-End Encryption: Data is transmitted via HTTPS and encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard.
  • Access Authorization: Only you have access to your data and analytical results.
  • Data Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete files and chat history at any time.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us via email: security@mia.ai
